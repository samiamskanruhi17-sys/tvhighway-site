Red Ball Discovery (Ages 5–8)

Utilizing 36-foot courts and low-compression red felt balls, this program focuses on early spatial perception, tracking, two-handed balance, and playful cooperative rally games.

Orange & Green Ball Intermediate (Ages 9–12)

Progression to 60-foot and standard 78-foot regulation courts. Students master topspin shape, directional rally discipline, approach shot transitions, and tactical court positioning.

High School Varsity Pipeline (Ages 13–18)

An intensive performance tier that serves as the official developmental pipeline for the Martha's Vineyard Regional High School varsity tennis teams. Includes match-situation simulations, video stroke breakdown, and USTA tournament travel schedules.

Multi-Sport Conditioning Clinics

Open athletic sessions offering speed, agility, and quickness (SAQ) drills for junior football, soccer, track, and rugby athletes to enhance lateral mobility and injury prevention.
