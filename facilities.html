Har-Tru Courts & Center Facilities

A purpose-built athletic campus situated on 11 acres of Oak Bluffs pine woodland.

Championship Har-Tru Red Clay Courts

Four precision subterranean-irrigated Har-Tru clay courts engineered for optimal slide and low impact.

Our center features four regulation Har-Tru green and red clay courts. Chosen specifically for junior athletic longevity, clay provides natural shock absorption that drastically reduces stress on growing knee and ankle joints, while forcing players to master strategic footwork, rally endurance, and point construction.

Year-Round Seasonal Air Dome (The Bubble)

To overcome New England's harsh coastal winters, our facility utilizes an inflatable climate-controlled dome erected over the courts from late autumn through spring. This ensures that youth instructional clinics, varsity training sessions, and open community court play continue uninterrupted twelve months a year.

The Chuck Sullivan Clubhouse

Designed by celebrated island architect Chuck Sullivan with interior aesthetics by Twanette Tharp, our clubhouse features spacious junior locker rooms, an upstairs tournament observation deck overlooking all four courts, audio-visual technical playback suites for stroke mechanics review, and administrative offices.

Outdoor Athletic Training Grounds

In addition to court space, the facility includes speed and agility conditioning corridors, ball machine practice cages, and multi-sport training turf utilized by local soccer, football, and track youth athletes for lateral conditioning.
