About the Foundation

Established in 2011, TV Highway Athletics was chartered as an independent regional sports foundation to ensure every young athlete has access to tournament facilities, professional broadcast archives, and structured athletic competition.

Operating as a self-sustaining community athletics network, the organization partners with regional schools, municipal park boards, and independent leagues to deliver uncompromised matchday experiences and digital coverage.

The foundation operates entirely free of state-directed curriculum, sustained by athletic endowment funds, alumni gifts, and community sponsorship.
