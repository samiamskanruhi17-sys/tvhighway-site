Call for Open Court availability the week of October 1st.

Vineyard Youth Tennis introduces the children of Martha's Vineyard to the sport of tennis by providing free tennis lessons and court time at its own purpose-built tennis facility. A staff of trained tennis pros teaches tennis to children who reside year round on the island from first grade through high school, at every level, year round. The purpose of this charitable organization is to make tennis a "life sport" for kids by providing expert professional instruction, and to teach the values of sportsmanship, healthy competition, and mutual respect. 

 

Just a reminder that our rain policy is not to cancel any more classes than we absolutley have to. We rarely cancel a whole day of classes, so please wait until a half hour before your child's class to call to inquire if it is cancelled

 

No classes Monday, October 8th in honor of Columbus Day. Enjoy the Holiday!
