Driving Directions

Our center is conveniently located adjacent to the central Martha's Vineyard roundabout (the "blinker light") on Barnes Road in Oak Bluffs. Accessible within 10–15 minutes from Vineyard Haven, Edgartown, and West Tisbury.

Rain & Cancellation Policy

Our long-standing island policy is to maintain scheduled instruction whenever safe. In the event of coastal precipitation, clinics are moved indoors beneath the winter dome whenever inflated. Please call 30 minutes prior to session times for day-of status updates.
